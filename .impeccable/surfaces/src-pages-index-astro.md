# Surface: CAPITAL one-page sales site (src/pages/index.astro)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: companies choosing office/commercial space in Astana. Action: «Подобрать офис» → plan picker → request form / WhatsApp. Proof: renders, plans, presentation facts only; unknowns render as [ЗАПОЛНИТЬ]. Build path: code-led (no image generation used this session).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The facade is the page's grammar: the building's vertical fins become the page's thin vertical rules, its floors become the floor picker, its dusk light becomes the single accent. Refuses the real-estate template of hero, icon-card grid and stat tiles.

OWN-WORLD: Night-glass navy ground (#121a2e family) alternating with travertine stone (#e9e3d8) sections; one accent, window-lamp amber (#d6a15c), reserved for the action and the selected unit. Condensed light display (Alumni Sans) set large like tall glazing, neutral Cyrillic text face (Onest). Hairline rules, spec-sheet rows, full-bleed renders, no cards, no icons tiles.

STORY: The visitor sees the building at dusk, learns where (Bekturov first line, next to Khan Shatyr) and what (offices from 17 m² with finish, retail floor, handover Q1 2028, instalments), turns the model, picks a floor and a unit on the real plan, and sends a request or opens WhatsApp.

Lower left: CAPITAL set huge in condensed light, one line «Офисы от 17 м² на первой линии Бектурова», amber button «Подобрать офис», a quiet fact line (сдача I кв. 2028 · рассрочка до сдачи). Right edge: thin vertical fin rules rising on load.

FORM: Brief-pinned architectural premium real-estate site with the presentation's navy identity; seed: none (pinned) — "Стиль: архитектурный, как у сайтов премиальной недвижимости. Много воздуха, крупные фото на всю ширину, строгая типографика ... сдержанная палитра из 2–3 цветов, взятых с фото здания."
