## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: Astro (static output) + GSAP ScrollTrigger + Lenis + Three.js. The user named this stack ("Astro (или Next.js) + GSAP + Lenis + Three.js"); Astro was chosen because a one-page sales site is content-first, ships as static HTML, and deploys to Netlify or Vercel unchanged.

## Users

Companies and business owners choosing office or commercial space in Astana: tenants and buyers comparing business centres by location, floor area, finish and terms. They browse on phones and laptops, often between meetings, and decide to request a selection or a call.

## Product Purpose

CAPITAL is a business centre under construction: two sections, 9–14 floors, ground-floor commercial premises with separate entrances, and office floors handed over with finished interiors. The site presents the building and turns visits into enquiries ("Подобрать офис", form or WhatsApp).

## Positioning

First line of Bekturov Street on the Left Bank, next to Khan Shatyr, with an exit to Turan Avenue. Offices from 17 m² in fit-out-ready condition, so a 1–2 person firm and a full team can buy into the same address. The developer's own management company runs the building. Purchase by instalments until handover (Q1 2028).

## Operating Context

- Visitors compare several business centres and arrive from ads, maps, and links shared by brokers.
- Main enquiry channels: callback form and WhatsApp. Phone, WhatsApp number, email and social links are not yet provided.
- Sales office address in the presentation: г. Астана, пр. Кабанбай батыра, 58Б.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: CAPITAL, set as a wide geometric uppercase wordmark; descriptor "Бизнес-центр".
- Presentation identity: deep navy ground, white type, light-weight headings, thin vertical line motif echoing the facade fins.
- Tone: premium, calm, confident; short, lively copy like a good real-estate agency. No hype phrases, no mention of how the site was made.

## Product Principles

1. Every number on the site comes from the presentation or a plan; the rest is [ЗАПОЛНИТЬ].
2. The building sells itself: renders lead, copy stays short.
3. One action everywhere: pick an office (selection request or WhatsApp).
4. Calm over spectacle: motion is slow and architectural, never busy.
