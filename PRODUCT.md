# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: Astro, building static HTML. A marketing site is mostly content, so it should ship as fast static HTML with little or no client JavaScript. Astro components keep sections reusable, and the output can be hosted anywhere. No deploy target has been chosen yet.

## Users

Visitors arriving at a marketing / landing site. The success action is **sign up / start a trial**.

Open: who the primary visitor is (individual, small business, agency, team) and the situation they arrive in have not been confirmed.

## Product Purpose

A marketing / landing site for a product about **websites**. The site exists to turn visitors into sign-ups or trial starts.

Open: whether the product is a website builder, a web design service, or templates/themes has not been confirmed. Resolve this before writing any copy or surface strategy.

## Positioning

Open: not yet stated. Future work must not invent a differentiator.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Surface type: marketing / landing site (web).
- Primary conversion: sign up / start trial.
- Open: product name, feature set, pricing, and trial terms.

## Evidence on Hand

The user listed existing **sites** as available material. Most likely these are real websites made with or for the product, to show as examples. Their location, paths, and permission to show them have not been given.

Absent, and never to be fabricated: testimonials, customer names or logos, usage numbers, benchmarks, pricing, press, and awards.

## Product Principles

1. Every claim on the site must trace back to real material; open facts stay placeholders, not invented copy.
2. Real sites are the strongest proof: show actual work over descriptions of it.
3. One clear path to sign-up / trial; everything on the page supports that action.
